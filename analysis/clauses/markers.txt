# Clause markers: their words are counted with the clause they introduce, not
# with the clause above. One entry per line ('#' starts a comment); shell-style
# wildcards allowed. An entry is matched against
#   - lexical types of words: a matching word joins the nearest clause below the
#     node where it combines (complementizers take the clause as a complement);
#   - construction names: words in the non-clause daughter of a matching
#     construction join the clause in its other daughter.
cm_*              # complementizers: that, whether, if, for, to (lexical types cm_...)
mrk-nh_cl_c       # marker + clause construction
flr-hd_*          # filler-head constructions: the filler (relative or wh-phrase,
                  # topicalized phrase) belongs to the clause in the head daughter,
                  # whose gap it fills through SLASH
